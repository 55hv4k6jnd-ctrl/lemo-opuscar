# SIT MEN — treatment

Style: **Chinese Ink Wash** (`styles/ink-wash/STYLE.md`, kept whole), pushed dark: horror + martial action.
Original. The hero, **Sit Men**, is a stick-figure monk: a bald head with six ordination dots, a white headband with **one red dot** — the film's single vermilion (it rhymes with the seal). His enemies: the walking dead of a drowned valley, drawn as wet ink that walks. No gore: the dead dissolve back into ink.
No voice. Four short English inscriptions. Length ≈ 3 min. 1920×1080, 24 fps. The user asked for: an epic setting, many push-ins and pull-backs, cinematic camera, jump scares, smooth spectacular action.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A siege (one night, one temple).** He defends the temple gate against the horde until dawn. Pure action, but one location for three minutes, and no horror build.
2. **A descent (mountain → valley → back).** He goes down to a silent village, finds the dead, flees back up to the bell. Horror on the way down, action on the way up; the bell is a rule the film sets up and then breaks. **← chosen**
3. **A dream loop.** He meditates; each time he opens his eyes the dead are closer, until he can't tell waking from sleep. Strong dread, but little action.

## 2. Logline and arc

A monk who keeps the mountain's great bell walks down into a valley gone silent, and learns that the dead who fear the bell have stopped listening to it.

- **Setup**: a tiny figure meditating on a peak in an ocean of blank paper; the bell; the inscription "The dead fear the bell."
- **Descent (horror)**: crows that don't move; a bamboo forest at night seen only in his lantern's circle; a false alarm (a fox); a figure in the background that isn't there the second time; the village; a door that opens by itself; a slow push — the jump scare.
- **Action**: the staff fight in the village street; his hand bell freezes the dead (the rule works); the pull-back reveals the whole valley moving; he runs up the mountain; the great bell holds them (the rule works again).
- **Turn (rule violation)**: they keep coming through the sound. Silence. He sits — the one thing he knows. One stroke: the flying-white sweep of the staff splits the horde into ink.
- **Ending (unresolved)**: dawn, the valley clean paper. He bows. His shadow on the stone, a beat late, turns its head toward us. Three bell tolls in the dark.

## 3. Benchmark (grammar only)

- Ink-painting animation (blank paper as space, things painted into existence) for the look; wuxia fight choreography (stillness before the stroke, one decisive move) for action; slow-burn horror (negative space, background horror, sudden silence) for the scares. *Not taken*: any character, composition, choreography or melody.

## 4. Horror techniques used (from the user's list), shot by shot

Negative space (2, 7) · Fear of the unknown / restricted POV — the lantern circle (3) · False alarm → real threat (3) · Background horror + visual anomaly — the figure among the bamboo, gone on the second look (3) · Sudden silence (2, 6, 11) · Slow camera movement + delayed reveal → jump scare (5–6) · Repetitive sound motif — the three bell tolls (1, 9, 15) · Rule violation — the bell stops working (10–11) · Escalation — one, then ten, then the whole valley (6–9) · Unresolved mystery — the shadow (15).

# SWEEP — treatment (v2: 80s Cel Anime)

Style: **80s Cel Anime** (`styles/cel-anime-80s/STYLE.md`, kept whole). The style was changed from Chinese Ink Wash at the user's request; the story, the script and the 40 voice lines are unchanged (§1–2 below), the picture, colour, type, motion and sound follow the new style.
An original folk tale about a tiny, overlooked mountain imp, told as a late-80s fantasy OVA played back on tape. No characters, names, scenes, designs or music from any existing film.
Language: English narration and dialogue (Kokoro now; ElevenLabs takes replace it line for line later — `SCRIPT_ELEVENLABS.md`). Subtitles: tape subtitles (Barlow Semi Condensed 600, cream-yellow, thick dark outline). Length ≈ 5 min 20 s.

## 1. Three candidate structures (for this style)

1. **Mock opening credits** — the hundred demons introduced one by one with name cards, the imp last with an empty card. Funny and very of the era, but it spends the film on the demons.
2. **Two-hander** — a locked two-shot of the imp and the child talking under the pine, breaking into one storm sequence. Intimate, but the mountain world never opens up.
3. **One place, four colour models (the gate at morning, dusk, storm, the morning after) + the journey between. ← chosen.** The style's own move: the same painted composition of the cave gate returns in different colour models (色指定) — the routine (gold morning), the empty evening (violet dusk), the storm (indigo, lightning backlight), and the end (pink dawn, the King back, the imp now named). Between them: the demons' line-up, the pond at night, the flight, the child, the great sweep (speed grammar + backlit light), the lantern village.

## 2. Logline and arc

Unchanged: for three hundred years a tiny imp has swept the gate of a demon king's cave and nobody has asked his name — until a storm empties the mountain and leaves him alone with a lost human child. Setup (the gate) → turn (the storm, the silence, the one sweep) → ending (the name, the gate repainted).

## 3. Benchmark (grammar only)

- **Late-80s fantasy OVAs in general**: airbrushed skies and painted forests behind flat two-tone cels, backlit magic, held stills at the peak, tape texture. *Not taken*: any character, creature design, logo or shot.
- **80s anime opening sequences**: chrome title, a brass hit, a character pose on a held cel with a light sweep. *Not taken*: any title, melody or lettering.

## 4. Characters, colour models & the accent

- **Sweep (the imp)**: knee-high, a round plum-violet body with a cream belly, one curled horn, big almond eyes with two highlights; he carries a broom taller than himself. **Accent colour: vermilion** — his broom's tassel and a short scarf — it pops in every lighting set.
- **The Great King**: a huge indigo-black ogre with gold eyes, horns, a fur collar; cowardly.
- **The hundred demons**: flat silhouettes in teal, mustard, rust, olive — tusks, many eyes, fangs, a long neck.
- **The child**: a small girl in a straw hat, faded blue jacket, red-brown hair; drawn the 80s way (almond eyes, long thin brows).
- **Colour models**: *morning* (gold sky, sage cliffs, warm skin), *dusk* (violet, amber rim light), *night* (indigo, moon cyan, firefly gold), *storm* (navy, lightning white-cyan backlight, wet reflections), *dawn* (pink, peach, lantern gold), *village night-lanterns* (backlit paper lanterns, emissive).
- **Light**: backlit lanterns, the moon on the pond, lightning, and **the sweep itself as backlit light** — a band of gold light that cuts through the storm.
